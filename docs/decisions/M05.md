# M05 implementation decisions

M05 extends the accepted M03 exact-company website pass. It publishes job and public-activity claims only from pages and feeds fetched through `NetworkGateway` after the website has passed the exact target-organisation identity gate. Every available M05 claim carries target organisation number, stable subject ID, a field family and scope, page/feed hash evidence, source snapshot, and the deterministic site proof.

Job extraction accepts `JobPosting` structured data and explicitly marked visible `JobPosting` cards on the verified origin. If structured data identifies an employer, the identifier and name must match the target or its already verified brand. Location output is limited to locality, region and country. A same-host canonical link is required. `validThrough` in the past means closed; no job is marked open because a page exists. `posting_date` and `application_deadline` retain the source string, normalized UTC and detected precision. `not_available` is recorded for current jobs only when an exact-site careers page explicitly says there are no openings. No careers page or an unavailable/blocked page remains ambiguous.

The ATS policy is explicit and currently empty: no ATS host or acquisition mode is approved. Embedded or linked ATS origins are recorded without a request. Future enablement requires an allowlisted host, an approved public acquisition mode, the normal SSRF/redirect/robots/gateway checks and exact employer identity on a tenant-specific result. A shared-host listing by itself is not target proof.

Activity comes from RSS/Atom entries and article data/pages linked from the verified company site. Items require a source-provided publication date within the default 365-day evidence-cutoff window. The source date string, normalized UTC, precision, and `as_of` are retained. Sitemap `lastmod`, retrieval time, undated content and future-dated content do not become activity facts. Events deduplicate by target, normalized headline and publication day so syndicated copies with different URLs do not inflate event coverage. Summaries are extractive and contact-like PII is omitted.

No external job or activity provider is enabled. The starter Google News, LinkedIn guest, reviews, YouTube search and Maps connectors remain quarantined because their current rights/acquisition/reproducibility modes are not approved. Company-owned public extraction follows the documented transient factual-extraction policy, skips a source when its terms prohibit the use, and records attribution provenance. Site-specific terms can remain unreviewed where no prohibition is observed; this is reported rather than represented as a license.

The M04 accepted-domain set and exact-org proof rules are unchanged. M05 does not change search's disabled state, candidate-only behavior, output row cardinality, canonical envelope, or deterministic publication boundary. Company-owned social/profile URLs continue through the M03 inventory path; M05 does not crawl third-party profiles.
